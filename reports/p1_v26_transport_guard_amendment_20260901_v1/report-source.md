# P1 prospective transport-guard amendment from v26

This amendment is prospective and not a v24 rescue. It uses no source row, target, fit, lock, artifact, official/test/sample/submission/hidden file, CSV, or upload.

The old veto counted `(station, layer, chronological_half)` environments. In v24, S-ORS layer 5 in half 0 and the same S-ORS layer 5 in half 1 counted as two environments and both had inner precision 1.0. The untouched outer action nevertheless moved to G-ORS/I-ORS and all 332 additions were false positives. The structural gap is therefore identity diversity, not merely chronological diversity.

For every new experiment from v26 onward, a threshold must retain the existing pooled Wilson-90 LCB and minimum-addition rules and also satisfy all of the following before outer labels are opened:

- both chronological halves are represented;
- at least two distinct station-layer identities are represented;
- at least two distinct stations are represented;
- every supported environment has at least 10 proposals, positive TP count, and precision strictly above 0.55.

The amendment does not change or rerun v24 and cannot justify an execution without a separately novel information/objective axis.
