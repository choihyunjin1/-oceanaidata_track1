## Reconciliation rule

Where sources disagree with a stored numeric gate, the stored historical result is preserved and only its future interpretation changes. No threshold is weakened after seeing a candidate, no old one-shot attempt is reopened, and no favorable exposed-surface result is called fresh confirmation.
