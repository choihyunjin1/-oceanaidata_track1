# P1 v48 identity-routing root-cause audit

Audience: P1 experiment review. Date: 2026-09-01 KST. Scope: repository-wide negative fingerprint, already exposed P1 calibration receipts, and target-free `station/layer/time` occupancy from the authorized train prefix. No new labels, outer windows, official files, or hidden data were opened.

## Direct answer

v48 is closed at zero fits. The recurring failure is not a lack of station-layer rows: the causal prefix contains 294,278 rows across 11 station-layer cells, with at least 1,971 rows per cell. Instead, multiple otherwise high-precision objectives collapse their highest-LCB actions to one station, one layer, and one chronological half. The direct intervention—identity-routed experts—already exists in the P1 registry as `station_routed_binary_experts_lgbm`, so executing another identity expert is an exact-family repeat.

The closest modern alternative was Expert-Choice mixture routing. Zhou et al. describe expert-choice routing as fixing expert capacity to address expert under- or over-specialization ([NeurIPS 2022 primary paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/2f00ecd787b432c1d36f3de9800728eb-Abstract-Conference.html)). That mechanism balances expert utilization, not station-layer anomaly actions. Target-free row occupancy cannot certify that its high scores or true positives would cover multiple identities, which is the actual v28 gate. Treating capacity balance as identity-action support would therefore overstate the source and bypass the prospective safety veto.

## Evidence and limitations

The highest-LCB candidates in v36r1, v39, v46, and v47r1 each supported exactly one station-layer identity and only half-1. v38 showed that broad apparent station support can also hide zero-TP cells, while v34/v37 showed that diversity without precision is insufficient. This is evidence for score/action concentration on the exposed historical surface, not proof of future or official behavior.

Repository fingerprinting also separated and froze adversarial station invariance, Barlow redundancy reduction, Fishr gradient variance, AND-mask, spectral decoupling, and MixStyle. None provides a target-free certificate of identity-level action opportunity. The proposed four-expert/top-1 recipe was fixed before rejection, but was not executed because it was both directly duplicated at the identity-expert level and unidentifiable at the required action-support level.

The next research gap is a causal, label-free observable that predicts prospective station-layer anomaly-action opportunity. The current train-only schema establishes occupancy but not where precise anomaly additions will occur. Until such a proxy is scientifically justified, another router, loss, score-balancer, or threshold change would be a retune rather than new information.
